Evolutionary Biology (BIOS 3600/BIOL 6600)

An introduction to evolutionary biology for intermediate undergraduates and beginning graduate students. The course develops population thinking and the ability to read scientific literature, and covers the history of life, major evolutionary transitions, speciation, phylogenetics, natural and sexual selection, genetic drift, genome evolution, evo-devo, and experimental evolution.

Microbiology Lab (BIOS 3381)

A lab course on commonly used microbiological techniques: field sampling, cultivation and enumeration of microorganisms, enrichment and isolation, microbial growth, antibiotic resistance and antibacterial activity, DNA extraction, whole-genome sequencing, and bioinformatic analysis.

Communicating Biological Research (BIOS 4460)

A course on oral and poster presentations. Students learn to present and critically discuss their own research results, engage an audience, and give constructive feedback on their classmates’ talks.
